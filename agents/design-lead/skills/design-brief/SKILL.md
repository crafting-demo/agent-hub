# Design brief

1. Draft the thesis: mood sentence, 4-6 hex palette, at most two typefaces,
   layout concept with an ASCII wireframe, one signature element.
2. Critique it. If it could be any product in the category, revise.
3. Write `DESIGN.md`: locked visual system, copy tone, motion budget,
   accessibility floor.

## Do not

- Use Inter, Roboto, or the system stack as the personality face
- Reach for the common AI defaults (cream plus terracotta, acid green on
  near-black, generic purple SaaS) unless the brief asked
- Implement product features or APIs
